FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about RS SHOP and our products.

Are all products 100% original?

Yes! Every single product at RS SHOP is 100% original and sourced from trusted suppliers. We never compromise on quality or authenticity.

How can I place an order?

You can place an order directly through our website by adding items to cart, or you can message us on WhatsApp at 01996283245 and we'll help you order manually.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bKash, Nagad, Rocket, and Cash on Delivery (COD). All mobile banking payments go to the number 01996283245.

Do you deliver all over Bangladesh?

Yes! We deliver all over Bangladesh. Delivery charge is ৳60 for most areas. Delivery typically takes 2–4 business days depending on your location.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 3-day easy return policy. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, contact us within 3 days of receiving your order and we'll sort it out for you.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 2–4 business days across Bangladesh. For orders within Purbadhala, same-day or next-day delivery may be available.
