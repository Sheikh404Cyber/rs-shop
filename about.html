WHO WE ARE

Crafting Style Since 2021

RS SHOP was born from a simple belief — that every person deserves to wear quality without compromise. Located in the heart of Purbadhala, we started as a small corner shop with a big dream: to bring premium watches and sunglasses to our community at honest prices.

Over the years, we have grown into a trusted name for hundreds of happy customers across the region. Every product we carry is carefully selected for its quality, durability, and style — because we believe what you wear reflects who you are.
